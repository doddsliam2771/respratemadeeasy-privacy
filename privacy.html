Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Resp Rate Made Easy Apple Watch application.

Disclaimer

This application is intended for informational and educational purposes only.

The app allows users to manually count breaths and estimate breaths per minute by tapping the screen, learn ECG (electrocardiogram) rhythm recognition through quizzes, and use educational tools such as scoring systems (e.g., GCS) and clinical learning features.

This app does not provide medical advice and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical judgment, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you have concerns about your breathing, heart rhythm, neurological status, or overall health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Information Collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

No personal data, health data, ECG recordings, scoring inputs (including GCS), location information, or identifying information is collected.

All functionality, including ECG learning content, quiz progress, and scoring tools, operates locally on your device and does not require an internet connection.

Subscriptions

The app offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions to access premium content, including extended ECG quiz material and additional educational features.

Subscriptions are processed securely through Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment details, billing information, or Apple ID data.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data Security

Since the app does not collect or store data, no personal information is stored or transmitted.
